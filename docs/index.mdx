Yellowhammer runs local, unattended engineering Nights on your Mac. A [Night](/reference/statuses-and-glossary#night) selects one [Feature](/reference/statuses-and-glossary#feature) for a [Project](/reference/statuses-and-glossary#project), coordinates work through Linear, and leaves pull requests for you to review and merge.
