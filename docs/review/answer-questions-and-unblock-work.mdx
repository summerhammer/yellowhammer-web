A Work Card may enter Waiting on You when agent work needs a decision or answer. Read its recorded reason first: some conditions need an edit or configuration change instead of a reply.

<DocumentationOutline />

## Goal

Decide whether a Work Card needs an answer, an edit, or a configuration change.

## Before you begin

Open the Work Card from the Night Summary, Pulse, or Linear. Read its status and recorded Block Reason before taking action.

## Steps

### Identify the request

Summarize the specific question or missing decision. Distinguish an agent question in Waiting on You from a refusal that points to scope, permissions, or Protected Paths.

### Take the matching action

Describe where a human answer belongs and when the underlying Work Card or Project configuration must be edited instead. Verify supported gestures in the current app and Linear workflow.

### Recheck the Work Card

Explain how to confirm the response or change was recorded and whether the Work Card can proceed on a later Night.

## Confirm it worked

Name the updated record and the visible state or activity that confirms the human action was captured. Do not promise an immediate retry unless the product guarantees it.

## Common problems

Cover replying to a refusal instead of fixing its cause, editing the wrong Project's configuration, and a Work Card that remains blocked after a response. Link the diagnostic record.
