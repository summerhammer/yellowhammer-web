## The core objects

A [Project](/reference/statuses-and-glossary#project) groups one product's repositories, specification source, Linear project, and configuration. A [Feature](/reference/statuses-and-glossary#feature) describes an intended outcome. Its [Work Cards](/reference/statuses-and-glossary#work-card) carry the repository-scoped pieces of work.

A [Night](/reference/statuses-and-glossary#night) is a scheduled window of activity for a Project. One Feature is in flight at a time, but several can land sequentially during the same Night. Landed Features can remain in the unmerged stack while you review their pull requests.

## Read evidence at the right level

Use a Work Card's Attempts, review Rounds, and Checks to investigate an individual piece of work. Use the Feature's Definition of Done and Verification report to evaluate the intended outcome across repositories. Use the Night Summary for what happened during that Night, including failures and decisions still waiting.

Yellowhammer coordinates agents and records their results. You make triage decisions in Linear and merge decisions in GitHub.
