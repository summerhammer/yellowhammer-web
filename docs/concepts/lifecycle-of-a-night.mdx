A Night selects one Feature, dispatches its Work Cards to local agent routes, evaluates completion, and reports outcomes for human review. The exact internal phases can vary by product version.

## From specification to selection

The specification describes product intent and completion evidence. The Night selects one Feature for its Project; do not imply it selects several Features at once.

## Local execution

Work Cards are dispatched to configured agent Routes in Orca worktrees on the Mac. Agent CLIs perform code changes; Yellowhammer coordinates and records their results.

## Verification and reporting

Verification reports against Definition of Done clauses and specification citations. The Night Summary carries the result, including empty or interrupted Nights.

## Human review

The human reviews Linear records and GitHub pull requests, answers questions, and decides whether to merge. Yellowhammer never merges.
