Each Yellowhammer [Night](/reference/statuses-and-glossary#night) belongs to one Project, with local tools executing its author, build, and land Acts. Different Projects can run independent Nights concurrently. Prepare the execution settings before leaving a Night unattended.

## A Night at a glance

The author Act selects and prepares a Feature. The build Act coordinates its Work Cards and review. The land Act verifies and publishes eligible results as pull requests. A Project has one Feature in flight at a time, but can advance through multiple Features sequentially in the same Night. The Night Summary reports the result, including Nights that produced no work.

The installed LaunchAgents invoke the engine independently of the app. Quitting Yellowhammer does not stop a Night; keeping the Mac available is still necessary. Yellowhammer leaves merging to you.
