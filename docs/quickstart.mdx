## What you will do

Use this path to understand the prerequisites, prepare one Project, then follow a Night through to its morning result. Acme Checkout is the illustrative product used in examples.

## Before you begin

Review [Requirements](/start/requirements) first. This page is a learning path; the linked setup pages hold topic-specific briefs until product procedures are verified.

## Steps

### 1. Prepare one Project

Follow the [setup overview](/setup/overview): connect [Linear](/setup/connect-linear), [GitHub](/setup/connect-github), [Orca and agent CLIs](/setup/connect-orca-and-agent-clis), and [add a Project and repositories](/setup/add-project-and-repositories). Prepare a [specification](/setup/prepare-specification) with an observable Definition of Done.

### 2. Rehearse and prepare to schedule

Read about [rehearsing](/run/rehearse-before-scheduling), [scheduling](/run/configure-the-schedule), [Routes](/run/choose-agents-and-routes), and [limits](/run/set-limits-and-protected-paths) before unattended execution. Verify exact controls and prerequisites against the current app.

### 3. Review the morning

Read the [Night Summary](/review/read-the-night-summary), find the selected Feature and Work Cards in Linear, then inspect any repository pull requests before deciding whether to merge.

## Confirm it worked

You can identify the selected Feature, find its Work Cards and Night Summary in Linear, and review any pull request before deciding whether to merge. The exact success indicators in the app need release verification.
